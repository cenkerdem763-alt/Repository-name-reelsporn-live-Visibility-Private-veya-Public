## Gig Description

```text
Do customers message your business on WhatsApp, ask for prices, and then disappear?

I will write a ready-to-use WhatsApp Business message system for your small business so you can reply faster, sound more professional, and guide customers toward booking, buying, or taking the next step.

This service is useful for:

- Beauty salons
- Hair salons
- Clinics
- Coaches and consultants
- Fitness studios
- Restaurants
- Local service businesses
- Boutiques
- Real estate agents
- Car services

Depending on your package, I can write:

- Welcome message
- Pricing inquiry reply
- Appointment booking message
- Appointment confirmation
- Promotion message
- Follow-up for undecided customers
- Returning customer message
- Complaint / unhappy customer reply
- No-show follow-up
- Upsell / add-on service message
- WhatsApp Business quick replies
- Short usage guide

Every message is customized to your business, tone, services, and customer journey.

Tone options:

- Friendly
- Professional
- Premium
- Warm and helpful
- Direct and sales-focused

Important:
I do not write misleading claims, medical guarantees, fake reviews, spam messages, or anything that violates platform rules. The goal is clear, ethical, useful customer communication.
```
